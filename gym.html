About KIET Galaxy Gym

Welcome to KIET Galaxy Gym—the ultimate campus fitness hub. We provide a clean, secure, and fully equipped space tailored for hostel students to balance academics with peak physical wellness.

Our facility features state-of-the-art resistance machines, dedicated free-weight zones, and high-performance cardio equipment designed to push your limits safely.
